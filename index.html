About NSS at NIET

Empowering Students Through Community Service

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a Central Sector Scheme of the Government of India, under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. It was launched in 1969 with the primary objective of developing the personality and character of student youth through voluntary community service.

At NIET, the NSS unit has been instrumental in bridging the gap between educational institutions and the community. Our volunteers actively participate in various programs aimed at rural development, environmental conservation, health awareness, and educational initiatives.

The NSS motto "NOT ME BUT YOU" embodies the essence of selfless service and reflects the idealism that drives every NSS volunteer to work tirelessly for the betterment of society.
